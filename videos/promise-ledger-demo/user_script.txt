# Demo video script (aim for 4 minutes, hard limit 5)

Record at 1280x720 or larger. Have the app open and the example email ready.

| Time | On screen | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Title card or the ledger header | Fieldday is five people running a 15,000-a-day festival. For ten weeks they say yes: in emails, on calls, in contracts. Nobody writes those promises down in one place. |
| 0:25 | The summary line: 39 promises, 25 conversations, 6 can't all be kept | We gave it 25 real-looking conversations. It found 39 promises. Six of them can't all be kept. |
| 0:45 | Click "Crew parking passes: 41 promised, 30 exist" | Five different people each handed out a few parking passes. Every one was reasonable. Together it's 41 passes for a 30-space lot. |
| 1:10 | Click "See where" on one promise | Every promise links back to the exact sentence it came from, so you never have to trust the AI's word for it. |
| 1:30 | Click the green room clash | Priya gave Marlo Vance the green room from 5:30. Sam gave Halfmoon Parade the same room from 6. Neither knew. |
| 1:50 | Click the set length clash | The contract says 60 minutes. A friendly email a week later says 75. The artist will hold us to the email. |
| 2:10 | Click the vegan caterer clash | And this one: "we'll sort a vegan caterer." Promised by the director, owned by nobody. |
| 2:30 | Click "Add a conversation", "Use an example", "Read it" | Now a new email comes in. Mei promises a buggy for Saturday. All four buggies were already promised. |
| 2:55 | The new clash appears, buggies bar turns red | The ledger catches it the moment it's said, not on show day. |
| 3:10 | Click "Draft a fix" on any clash | It drafts the awkward email for you. It never sends anything. |
| 3:30 | Scroll to the table, filter by one person | How it works: the AI only reads and extracts. Plain code does the counting and decides what clashes, so a hallucination can't invent a problem or hide one. |
| 3:50 | Back to the top | The Promise Ledger. Know what you've said yes to. |

After recording the paste step, click "Remove pasted" to reset before any retake.
